# Spotify

Everything here is optional and needs **Spotify Premium**. Sign in once under **Settings → Spotify**.

## What you can do
- **Import** playlists and your listening history (**Add → Import from Spotify**). Public playlist links need no sign-in; your own playlists use the sign-in; Spotify's "Account data" download brings in everything including your play history.
- **Play Spotify tracks you do not have as files.** TurboMusic sends the song to a Spotify device you choose in the cast list: the Spotify app on your phone or PC, a smart speaker, or Soloist. TurboMusic's queue and "play B after A" rules still decide what comes next. Spotify plays the audio itself, so TurboMusic's equaliser and offline copies do not apply to those tracks.
- **Discover** suggests artists from your ratings using open music data, and finds them on Spotify.

## Ways to play
1. **The Spotify app you already have** (easiest). TurboMusic is a remote control for it.
2. **Play inside this app** (browser and some desktop setups). Turns the page into a Spotify device. It needs a secure page (https or localhost) and a browser with DRM support, so it is **not offered in the desktop app or the Android app**.
3. **Soloist on a Raspberry Pi.** Spotify's headless player runs on the server and plays through that machine's speakers; TurboMusic can also stream its sound to your apps. Setup is under *Settings → Spotify → Soloist*.

## If devices are missing
Press *Check the connection* in the Spotify tab: it shows what Spotify answered. The most common cause is signing in with a different Spotify account than the one that owns the speaker.
